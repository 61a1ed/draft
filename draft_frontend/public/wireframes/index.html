Mana Curve Minimap — 5 Interactive Wireframes

Each wireframe below is an interactive prototype with a live slider so you can test how the Mana Curve Minimap visualizes the draft pool draining from Round 1 to the end of the draft.

New · 5 Compact Draft Trackers

Unintrusive Live Draft Trackers (Light & Dark)

Interactive comparison of 5 compact tracker placements (Topbar Pill, Slim Ribbon, Sidebar Card, Floating Corner HUD, and Page-Heading Bar) for non-draft screens, with a live toggle for "Other Team" vs. "⚡ YOUR TURN!".

Snake Draft Board (Light & Dark Mode)

A Round × Team snake draft grid showing completed picks, the glowing "On the Clock" active cell, "On Deck" indicator, directional snake arrows, and high-contrast Light/Dark mode switch.

Open interactive prototype → Option 1 · Sticky Filter Bar

Scrollbar & Viewport Lens Minimap

Sits as a 36px strip at the bottom of the frozen filter bar. Shows solid remaining bars vs. hatched drafted bars, plus a sliding lens that tracks and jumps your horizontal scroll position on the Big Board.

Open interactive prototype → Option 2 · Header Sparkline

"Waterline Erosion" Silhouette

Embeds a smooth area chart in the Draft Board header. Compares the dashed "Initial Pool Roof" against the live "Remaining Waterline" and tracks the shifting Average Point Cost of remaining Pokémon.

Open interactive prototype → Option 3 · Right Sidebar Card

Sidebar Pacing & Budget Wall

Places a compact mana curve right above Your Roster in the right sidebar, complete with a dynamic "Max Cap Wall" and "Avg Pts/Slot Pace Line" that shift as you spend points.

Open interactive prototype → Option 4 · Discrete Blocks

Segmented "Equalizer" Heatmap

Each Pokémon is an individual LED block in its point-cost stack. As picks happen, blocks switch off and tiers shift color from Healthy Green to Thinning Amber to Critical Red.

Open interactive prototype → Option 5 · Corner Overlay

Floating Corner Radar + Scrubber

A collapsible bottom-right Picture-in-Picture radar that takes zero layout space and includes a Round 1 → Live scrubber to replay how the board drained.
